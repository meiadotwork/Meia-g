# Statement

Meia G's practice treats perception as a constructed condition rather than a passive act. His paintings function as spatial systems in which reflection operates as a mechanism, not an effect. Central to the work is chrome, a surface without intrinsic identity that exists only through its surroundings. What appears is not depicted but produced through the interaction between object, environment, and viewer. The image becomes unstable, contingent, and variable. These works do not represent; they generate perceptual situations where visibility is inseparable from context, and the viewer is implicated as an active element within the field.
