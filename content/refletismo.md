# Refletismo

## 2026

Meia rejects painting as image. Under the framework of Refletismo, his work dismantles representation and replaces it with conditions of perception. These paintings do not depict; they operate. What appears is not an image but the result of a system in which visibility is produced, destabilized, and made contingent.

Chrome is deployed as a material without identity. It carries no colour, no fixed form, no symbolic content. It exists only through what surrounds it. In doing so, it collapses the distinction between object and environment, forcing the work into a state where nothing is autonomous and everything is dependent. The image cannot stabilize because it is never fully there.

The viewer is not external to this system. Their presence alters the reflected field, implicating them directly in the construction of what is seen. Perception is no longer passive; it becomes an active variable. The work refuses distance. It produces a condition in which seeing is unstable, incomplete, and constantly shifting.

Set against controlled black grounds, the reflective surfaces intensify the tension between flatness and depth, clarity and distortion. These are not compositional decisions but structural ones. The work does not resolve contradiction; it sustains it. There is no narrative, no symbolism, no expressive gesture to decode. The painting offers no image to interpret.

Refletismo does not extend painting — it displaces it. What remains is not representation but condition. Not image, but fact.
